MAME Home Systems

Inputs

Inputs available to the MAME Home Systems core, in the order they appear in a movie file. Click a column heading to sort. Click again to reverse.

About This Data

1938 buttons and 66 axes, in the same order as the config. That order is the column order of a movie file. Order starts at 1 for each type. Mnemonic is the short letter for the button. Column is filled only when the config gives one. Buttons have no Min, Max or Neutral. Empty values are written as an empty string.

Controllers

Open a system to see its controller. Move the pointer over a button to see its row from the Inputs grid.

Atari 7800 (NTSC), Atari 7800 (PAL)

Inputs with no place on this picture are Reset, Select, Pause. Move the pointer over a button, or tab to it, to see its row in the grid below.

Intellivision

Inputs with no place on this picture are Lower-Left, 40 keyboard keys, SHIFT, Space, Cursor Up ^, Cursor Left %, Cursor Down Question, Cursor Right '. Move the pointer over a button, or tab to it, to see its row in the grid below.

Vectrex

Inputs with no place on this picture are Button 5, Lightgun X, Lightgun Y. Move the pointer over a button, or tab to it, to see its row in the grid below.

Odyssey 2 (US), Videopac G7000 (Europe), Videopac C52 (France)

Inputs with no place on this picture are Reset, 38 keyboard keys, Clear, Y / Yes, Enter, N / No, Question, Space, Divide, Times. Move the pointer over a button, or tab to it, to see its row in the grid below.

Move the pointer over a button, or tab to it, to see its row in the grid below.
