SDLPoP

Options

Settings the SDLPoP core exposes in the New Project wizard. Click a column heading to sort. Click again to reverse. Settings that are identical on every machine are listed once.

Settings

59 of 59
Version1.0, 1.1, 1.3, 1.41.0Which release of Prince of Persia is played. The project supplies that release's files. 1.0 (1990) and 1.1 (the IBM PC release) have the same data files. 1.3 (1992) and 1.4 (the Prince of Persia Collection CD) also have the same data files as each other. Those differ from the earlier ones in the level colours (the dungeon of level 3 is green, not blue), two sounds and the music. The guards fight by the tables of 1.0 in 1.0, and by the later tables in 1.1, 1.3 and 1.4. The copy protection asks from each release's own manual (one for 1.0, one for 1.1, and one for 1.3 and 1.4). Each release's PRINCE.EXE is checked too, because it is the file that tells the two releases of a pair apart.
Enable Cheatstrue, falsefalseStarts the game with its cheat word, as the DOS game was started from the command line (megahit, or improved for 1.3 and 1.4). The cheat buttons exist only when this is on. It also lets Next Level (Shift+L) skip any level without cutting the time to 15 minutes.
Player Name (Hall of Fame)Any textChimeraThe name that a won game enters in the hall of fame when its time earns a place there. It is entered as the player would have typed it. Only printable characters are used, and only as many as fit in the box (24 at most). The controls have no letter keys, so the game enters the name by itself.
Sound carddigital, pcSpeakerdigital'digital' is the Sound Blaster's digitized sound effects and the AdLib music, as SDLPoP plays them. 'pcSpeaker' is the PC speaker's sounds. The game waits for some sounds to end (the music at the end of a level, the title). So it is part of the machine, and a movie needs the same value. 'digital' needs the DIGISND and MIDISND files.
Random seed0 to 21474836470The starting value (seed) of the game's random number generator. The DOS game took it from the clock. A movie records the number it ran with.
Copy protectiontrue, falsetrueThe original game's copy protection. After level 1 comes the potions level, where the prince must drink the potion whose letter the manual gives. It is on in 1.0. SDLPoP turns it off by default.
First level1 to 141The level a new game starts on. The original game starts on 1.
Skip the titletrue, falsefalseStarts the first level immediately and does not play the title sequence.
Minutes-1 to 3276760The minutes on the clock at the start. The original game has 60. -1 means no time limit.
Hit points1 to 103The hit points at the start of the game. The original game has 3.
Most hit points1 to 1010The most hit points the prince can reach with life potions. The original game has 10.
Fixes and enhancementstrue, falsefalseSDLPoP's fixes for the original game's bugs, and its gameplay enhancements. Off is the original game. On applies those of the settings below that are turned on (all of them are off by default).
Crouch after climbingtrue, falsefalsePressing down and forward together right after climbing up makes the prince crouch. In the original he always climbs back down.
Freeze time during the end musictrue, falsefalseThe clock stops while a level's ending music plays. In the original it keeps running.

About this data

59 settings, 236 rows. There is one row for each machine and each option. A setting for several machines is repeated for each machine. A setting with no rule is listed under every machine as 'Always shown'. Origin says where the setting came from.
